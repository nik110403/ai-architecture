# Contributor guidance

If you were pointed here to evaluate this collection or adopt parts of it, not to change it, skip this file and start at README.md, section "For AI agents reading this repository". It gives the reading order, where each kind of fact lives, and the read-only prompt in "Use with your AI".

Work only on the requested component. Read the complete target and its local assets before editing. Check every affected behavior with the offline validator and focused tests. Do not assume a role declaration grants tools, permissions, isolation, or enforcement; the active harness and project rules decide that.
