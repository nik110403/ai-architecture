# Dependency and license disposition

The contract implementation uses only Python's standard library. `sources.json`
records portable identifiers and the limited source-resolution result. A declared
package is not adapter evidence. Unknown or partial asset licenses block
redistribution of that asset, while independently authored generic contracts may
remain distributable under this collection's license.

Untested means support is not claimed until an execution receipt exists. Three
sources are pinned in `sources.json` with origin, version and license:
`opencode-plugin`, `superpowers` and `document-skills`. The other four have no
selected source and no pin: `epic-infographics` (repository unresolved, package
metadata only) and `local-adapters` are excluded, and `research-project` and
`research-synthesis` are staged and inactive. No source is cleared for
redistribution here.
